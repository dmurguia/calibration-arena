# Marketing assets

Copied from the approved Calibrated brand kit merged into this repository. Canonical guidance and rights documentation: `brand/calibrated/README.md`, `DESIGN-BRIEF.md`, `RIGHTS.md`, `images/manifest.json`.

- The wordmark and C are the supplied outlined vectors. Their colors and geometry are preserved.
- IBM Plex Sans Regular/Medium and IBM Plex Mono Regular use bundled WOFF2 files. OFL notices are included beside them.
- Open sketch, working notation, instrument steel and paper/cloth are existing generated illustration/material studies, not photographs of employees, facilities or customer work.
- `share-card.svg` is a new code-composed layout using the approved vector identity and outlined IBM Plex Sans; `share-card.png` is its 1200 × 630 raster export. The reproducible layout script is `marketing/scripts/generate-share.py`.
- No reference-site artwork, customer logo, stock photograph or third-party UI screenshot is redistributed. The affiliation marks below identify founder-supplied team education and previous experience, not customers or institutional partnerships.

## About page affiliation marks

Downloaded October 13, 2026; original artwork and proportions retained.

- `affiliations/stanford.png`: black wordmark from [Stanford’s identity guide](https://identity.stanford.edu/visual-identity/stanford-logos/wordmarks/), original asset `http://identity.stanford.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2020/06/wordmark-nospace-black.png`.
- `affiliations/mit.svg`: standalone MIT mark extracted unchanged from the footer SVG on [MIT’s homepage](https://web.mit.edu/). Paths render in their default black without the site’s external CSS.
- `affiliations/verkada.svg`: original [Verkada brand-site logo](https://cdn.verkada.com/image/upload/v1671057149/brand/verkada-logo.svg), referenced by its [brand guide](https://brand.verkada.com/design).

These marks remain the property of their respective institutions and company. Their use here records the team backgrounds supplied by the founder; it is not an endorsement or a claim that the marks are part of Calibrated’s own brand kit.
