# Privacy Policy

*Last updated October 19, 2026.*

Calibration Arena is operated by Corsac Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation doing business as Calibrated Co. ("Calibrated", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the service and what we do with it.

## What we collect

When you sign in, we store your email address. When you use the service, we store your submitted questions, text extracted from files you upload (file names and fingerprints, not the files themselves), your conclusions, judgments, notes and improvement reports, any background details you self-report, and usage events like page visits and timing.

## How we use it

To provide the service: saving your notebook, showing your own work back to you, and operating comparisons. To build rankings, statistics and research from judgments. To improve the service. And, under the license in the Terms of Use, to license or sell your questions, uploaded file text, notes and judgments, with your name, email address and account identifiers removed, to third parties including AI model providers, software vendors and researchers, who may use them to evaluate, benchmark and train AI models. The optional research setting in your profile records whether we may contact you about research follow-ups; it does not change the license.

## Who receives it

Clerk handles sign-in. Railway and Vercel host the service. OpenAI and Anthropic produce the model answers: your prompts and uploaded file text go to them, directly or through OpenRouter, and their own retention policies apply. Vercel Analytics measures site traffic. Third parties that license de-identified content from us keep and use it under their own terms; we do not control their retention or deletion.

## Cookies and local storage

We store a session token, your sidebar preference and a pending question in your browser. Clerk sets its own cookies for authentication.

## Social sign-in

If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your email address through Clerk, not your name, photo or contacts.

## Retention and security

We keep your notebook and account data while your account exists. De-identified content already shared under the license, or included in rankings and statistics, is kept after deletion. Access is limited to the Calibrated team. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

## Your rights

You can view your own data in your notebook, correct your profile, and ask us to delete your account and notebook by emailing help@corsac.ai. If you are in California, you have the right to know what we collect, to request deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Because we license de-identified content to third parties, California law may treat that as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; you can opt out of future sales or sharing by emailing help@corsac.ai. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If EU or UK data protection law applies to you, you may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability, and object to processing.

## Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 18.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions or requests: email help@corsac.ai.
