# Use and provenance

Prepared 21 September 2026 for Calibrated Co.

## Fonts

Archivo Black and the IBM Plex families are bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Original notices are in each family directory. Commercial website and logo use is permitted under those terms. Keep notices with redistributed font software; do not sell the fonts by themselves or remove the notices. Modified font software remains subject to OFL requirements. Outlined graphics do not require visitors to install a font.

- [Archivo Black source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/archivoblack)
- [IBM Plex source and license](https://github.com/IBM/plex)
- [IBM typeface documentation](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/typography/typeface/)
- fonts/sources.json records download URLs and SHA-256 hashes.

No commercial font files are included.

## Images and identity

The five active images in images/masters were generated for this exploration with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. They are working assets for the Calibrated website and related communications, with no separate stock-image fee. Four prompts are recorded in images/prompts.json; the earlier approved Open sketch has a provenance note rather than a reconstructed prompt.

The C is a custom vector drawing developed from the approved pen/power-button concept. The wordmark uses Archivo Black; the regular tagline uses IBM Plex Sans. Both use outlined glyphs from the bundled OFL fonts. SVGs remain editable; their lettering is not generated raster text.

Keep the generated provenance in internal asset records; do not describe the illustrations as real photographs of your team or facility.

Reference screenshots, historical scans, watch-brand imagery and AfterQuery photographs are not included as website assets. Reference use does not supply redistribution rights. The production library contains generated originals only.

WebP exports change format and resolution only. Untouched PNGs remain as masters. This is a provenance record, not a replacement for the linked license and service terms.
