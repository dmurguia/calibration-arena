# Creative references

These guide the direction; they are not a stock-image download list.

- **Watchmaking:** hand adjustment, engraved scales, dark metal, brushed stainless steel and silver. Close material crops communicate precision without luxury-watch advertising.
- **1980s/90s tools:** instrument labels, carbon copies, fanfold paper, mono type and quiet monochrome labeling.
- **Pocket notebooks:** ivory paper, black book cloth, private shorthand, cross-outs and pressure. Moleskine belongs to its owner; no partnership is implied.
- **Leonardo's observations:** the method of finding a shared pattern in hair, water and motion, extended into contemporary drawing and code. Historical image reproductions require their own rights review.
- **[AfterQuery](https://www.afterquery.com/):** the user's reference for close, energetic editorial views of laboratories and machines. Its imagery and brand are not copied.
- **[IBM Plex](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/typography/typeface/):** the working and technical voice; Archivo Black is reserved for the identity. IBM Plex Sans Regular supplies conservative headings and notes. A restrained leather brown recalls a timeless watch strap.

**Open sketch → deliberate gesture → readable structure.** Human judgment remains visible in the final state.
